# How I add the next case

**Where it goes:** `index.html`, in the `#cases` section (Sheet 04.1). A commented card template sits at the bottom of that section.

**Shape of every case (three beats):**
1. **Problem** — what needed solving, and for whom.
2. **What I did** — the stack I chose, the decisions I made, the thing that broke.
3. **What came of it** — one honest win and one honest limitation.

## Steps
1. Open the Claude Project (it already knows my voice, stack, and identity kit) and have it interview me, one question at a time. The words stay mine.
2. Copy the commented template in `#cases`, fill in the three beats, link the repo or live URL.
3. In Sheet 04 ("What I'm Building"), change the project's badge from `IN PROGRESS` to `BUILT` and make sure its link points at the repo.
4. Commit and push from VS Code (Source Control → stage → message → commit → Sync). Netlify redeploys on push.
5. Paste the live URL into a share-preview checker (opengraph.xyz) and confirm the title, description, and image look right.
